# Fonts shipped with Switchboard

Each file here is a Latin subset of a variable font, weight 400 to 700, served
by `server.js` at `/fonts/<name>.woff2` and declared in `public/css/fonts.css`.
All of them are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1), which allows
bundling and redistribution with the software, and forbids selling the fonts
on their own. The full OFL text and each font's copyright line are in
`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` at the repository root (generated by
`scripts/notices.mjs`).

| File | Family | Role in Switchboard | Source project | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `fraunces-normal.woff2`, `fraunces-italic.woff2` | Fraunces (opsz 9 to 144, SOFT, WONK) | the display face before the HUD; not used since Nov 8 2026, kept until the next font clean-up | Undercase Type, https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces | OFL-1.1 |
| `spacegrotesk-normal.woff2` | Space Grotesk (weight 300 to 700) | display since the HUD (Nov 8 2026): the wordmark, page titles, card titles, the big numbers | Florian Karsten, https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk | OFL-1.1 |
| `publicsans-normal.woff2`, `publicsans-italic.woff2` | Public Sans | body text and buttons | U.S. General Services Administration (USWDS), https://github.com/uswds/public-sans | OFL-1.1 |
| `jetbrainsmono-normal.woff2` | JetBrains Mono | readouts, kickers, code, and the terminal | JetBrains, https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono | OFL-1.1 |

To replace or re-subset a file, keep the same name and weight range, or
change `fonts.css` to match. The bundled names stay fixed because the
terminal (`public/js/sessions.js`) waits for "JetBrains Mono" by name before
it measures its cells.
