Switchboard privacy

Updated November 6, 2026.

What Switchboard is

Switchboard is a console for your work: your mail, chats, calendar, builds, notes and agent sessions in one place. It runs on your own computer, or in your browser as the web board. Its sync service only ever holds data that is encrypted before it leaves your device.

Your data stays yours

  • Encrypted end to end. Your password makes the key that locks your data, on your device. The sync service stores only sealed data it cannot read, and nobody who runs it can read it either.
  • Connections are yours. When you connect Google, Microsoft 365 or GitHub, the sign-in tokens are sealed into your own encrypted account data. Your mail, chats and events are fetched straight from Google, Microsoft or GitHub to your computer or browser, shown to you, and not stored on any server of ours.
  • Shared only when you share. Nothing from your account reaches anyone else unless you share a project with them, and then only the kinds of things you chose.
  • No ads, no selling, no tracking. Switchboard does not sell, rent or share your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

Google user data

If you connect your Google account, Switchboard asks to read, send and organise your Gmail and to read and change your Google Calendar, so it can show them and act on your clicks: a reply is sent only when you press Send. This data is used only to provide those features to you. Switchboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. No human reads your Google data, except when you ask for help and show it yourself.

Removing your data

Disconnect a service in Settings, Account, and its tokens are deleted from your account. You can also remove Switchboard's access in your Google, Microsoft or GitHub account settings at any time. To delete your Switchboard account and everything in it, write to the address below.
