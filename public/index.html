    Where do I find the link?

    Google Calendar: on the web, Settings, pick the calendar on the left, then Integrate calendar: copy Secret address in iCal format.

    Outlook: Settings, Calendar, Shared calendars, Publish a calendar: pick it, Can view all details, Publish, then copy the ICS link.

    iCloud: in the Calendar app, share the calendar and turn on Public Calendar, then copy the link.

    School, team or club: look for "Subscribe", "iCal" or "Add to calendar".
