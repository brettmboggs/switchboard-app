# Switchboard: the vision

Your work has become a room full of voices. AI agents writing your code.
Builds passing and failing. Clients on Teams, email in three places, bills,
packages, the day's errands. Each one lives in its own app, and each one
quietly waits for you to notice.

**Switchboard is the one place they all report to.** One screen. One colour.
When something needs you, it turns orange, and you answer it right there:
approve an agent's request, reply to a client, see the build go green,
without opening the other app.

**Your AI doesn't just work for you. It works with you.** Your agents can read
your board, raise their hand when they're stuck, and hand work to each other.
A client's message lands beside the session working on their project, and one
click sends it to the agent.

**It runs on your computer, not ours.** Your passwords and mail never pass
through anyone else's server. Nothing is ever sent without your click. Every
computer you own shares one board, end-to-end encrypted.

Switchboard doesn't add another app to your day. It's the last one you open.

**Everything that needs you. Nothing that doesn't.**

## Who it is for

People who keep several AI agents working while also running a business or a
job: solo founders, freelancers and consultants who build with AI, and
small-business owners starting to. Not big company teams whose IT picks their
tools.

## What we lead with

Few things, each one huge:

1. Orange means it needs you: the one idea people remember.
2. Agents in a clean chat: Claude, Codex, Gemini and local models, their
   questions as buttons.
3. Agents talk back: a stuck agent puts a card on your board.
4. Private and everywhere: runs on your machine, nothing sent without your
   click, one board on every computer.

Inbox, calendar, bills, packages and chores show how far it reaches; they
support the story rather than lead it.

## What it will not become

- A cloud service that holds your mail, passwords or code.
- Something that sends, pays or replies on its own.
- A better agent manager and nothing else: the AI companies build those for
  free. Switchboard's edge is everything around the agents, together.
- Noisy: if everything is orange, nothing is.

## How it lasts

Built by someone who lives in it every day, shaped by the people who use it.
Real users before new features; what they ask for comes first, and features
that already exist are made to work together before new ones are added.
